# Pedro's Guitar Hub (v0.15)

Your all-in-one practice suite for guitarists and musicians.

**Live App**: [https://pedro-s-guitar-hub-dev.vercel.app](https://pedro-s-guitar-hub-dev.vercel.app)

## Features
- **Interactive Play Along Coach**: Practice directly with your guitar and microphone! Features real-time pitch and chord recognition (autocorrelation + 12-pitch chromagram). Choose between "Pause & Wait until I play it right" or "Keep going and mark missed notes in red".
- **Dual Speed & BPM Control**: Control song tempo by percentage multiplier or exact BPM with automated sync and 1-click restore to original tempo.
- **Guitar Pro (.gp) Reader**: Full studio-grade tablature and sheet music engine powered by open-source alphaTab. Open or drag & drop `.gp3`, `.gp4`, `.gp5`, `.gpx`, and `.gp` files with track selection, live HUD feedback, zoom, tempo slow-down (25%–125%), and interactive SoundFont polyphonic audio playback.
- **Interactive Tab Maker**: Full guitar tablature composer (inspired by TabMaker). Click on strings, write frets, insert hammer-ons (`h`), pull-offs (`p`), bends (`b`), slides (`/`, `\`), vibratos (`~`), dead notes (`x`), listen with audio playback, and export formatted ASCII tabs.
- **ASCII Tab Reader & Player**: Paste raw ASCII guitar tabs from the web to parse and synthesize audio.
- **Chromatic Tuner**: Clip-on style digital tuner with auto-correlation fundamental pitch detection, real-time LED cent arc meter, and in-tune indicator.
- **Compact Metronome**: Tap-ready BPM controller with smooth slider, audio clicks, and visual beat pulsation.
- **Session Timer / Stopwatch**: Millisecond-precision practice timer with Spacebar toggle.
- **Random Note Trainer**: Fretboard note recall generator highlighting sharps and flats in an alternating chess pattern.
